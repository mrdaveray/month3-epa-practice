12 open questions of the kind an assessor may ask about your own process mapping work. Answer about your real process, then check your answer against what a strong answer covers.

How to use this practice 1:1 with your trainer: your trainer asks the question, you answer out loud, then they ask the follow-up probe. Tick what you covered together. On your own: type your answer first, then reveal the checklist and follow-up, and mark yourself honestly. Aim for about 2 minutes per answer. Have your process map and evidence in front of you, as you would in the real discussion.
